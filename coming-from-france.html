🎯 Vos Avantages Uniques

Pourquoi les Citoyens Français Bénéficient d'Avantages Spéciaux

La France a des accords bilatéraux avec les États-Unis qui vous donnent accès à des options de visa exclusives non disponibles pour la plupart des pays

E-2 Treaty Investor Visa

France is an E-2 treaty country! This means you can invest in a US business (typically $100K-200K) and get a renewable visa to live and work in America indefinitely.

  • ✅ Renewable every 2-5 years
  • ✅ Spouse can work in the USA
  • ✅ Children can study in USA schools
  • ✅ No lottery or quota system

Visa Waiver Program (VWP)

French passport holders can travel to the USA for up to 90 days without a visa for tourism or business meetings (via ESTA).

  • ✅ Visit before immigrating
  • ✅ Explore cities and opportunities
  • ✅ Network with businesses
  • ✅ Quick online authorization

Strong Bilateral Relations

France-USA relations facilitate easier business dealings, recognition of French credentials, and cultural exchange programs.

  • ✅ French degrees recognized
  • ✅ Professional licenses transferable
  • ✅ Active French expat communities
  • ✅ Cultural understanding

Your Immigration Pathway Options

Multiple visa routes available specifically for French nationals

E-2 Treaty Investor Visa

Best for entrepreneurs and business owners

🇫🇷 EXCLUSIVE

Requirements:

  • ✅ French citizenship required
  • ✅ Substantial investment ($100K-$200K typical)
  • ✅ Active business (not passive investment)
  • ✅ Must own at least 50% of business
  • ✅ Detailed business plan required

Benefits:

  • 🎯 Renewable indefinitely (2-5 year increments)
  • 🎯 Spouse gets work authorization
  • 🎯 Children under 21 can live/study in USA
  • 🎯 No annual caps or quotas
  • 🎯 Path to green card possible

💡 Popular sectors: Restaurants, consulting, tech startups, retail, franchises, e-commerce, import/export, real estate services

H-1B Specialty Occupation Visa

For skilled professionals with US job offers

POPULAR

Best For:

  • 💻 Software engineers & developers
  • 🔬 Scientists & researchers
  • 💼 Finance professionals
  • 🎨 Designers & architects
  • ⚕️ Healthcare professionals

Key Facts:

  • ✅ Requires bachelor's degree minimum
  • ✅ US employer must sponsor
  • ✅ 3-year initial period, renewable to 6 years
  • ⚠️ Annual lottery system (85,000 visas/year)
  • ✅ Can lead to green card

L-1 Intracompany Transfer

Transfer from French company to US office

Perfect if your French employer has (or can establish) a US office. Transfer as manager/executive (L-1A) or specialized knowledge worker (L-1B).

✅ Advantage: No lottery, faster processing, can apply for green card while on L-1A

O-1 Extraordinary Ability

For individuals with exceptional achievements

For French nationals who have achieved national or international recognition in sciences, arts, business, education, or athletics. Examples: award-winning chefs, renowned artists, published researchers.

Evidence Needed:

Awards, publications, media coverage, high salary, membership in professional associations

Benefits:

No lottery, renewable indefinitely, fast processing, can bring team members

Direct Green Card Pathways

Permanent residency options for French nationals

EB-1 (Priority Workers)

For extraordinary ability, outstanding researchers, or multinational executives

No labor certification required, fastest path to green card

EB-2 NIW

National Interest Waiver for advanced degree holders whose work benefits USA

No employer sponsorship needed, self-petition possible

EB-5 Investor

$800K-$1.05M investment in US business creating 10+ jobs

Direct path to green card, includes family members

What French Expats Should Know

Key differences between France and USA to prepare for

🏥 Healthcare System

No Universal Healthcare

Unlike French Sécurité sociale, USA has private insurance. You MUST have health insurance.

Employer Coverage

Most companies provide health insurance. Cost: $200-500/month for families.

Higher Out-of-Pocket Costs

Expect copays, deductibles, and higher costs than in France.

Complete Healthcare Guide →

💼 Work Culture

Longer Hours

40-50 hour weeks are normal vs. 35-hour French workweek. Less vacation time (10-15 days vs. 25+ in France).

Direct Communication

Americans value straightforward, positive communication. Less formal than French business culture.

Networking is Essential

Professional relationships and networking drive career success more than in France.

Culture Adaptation Guide →

🎓 Education System

School Choice by Location

Public schools determined by home address (unlike French centralized system). Choose your neighborhood carefully!

Expensive Universities

College costs $20K-70K/year vs. nearly free in France. Start saving early!

Extracurriculars Matter

Sports, clubs, volunteering crucial for college admissions (different from French baccalauréat focus).

Schools & Education Guide →

💰 Cost of Living

Housing Costs Vary Widely

NYC/SF: $3K-5K/month for 2BR. Austin/Dallas: $1.5K-2.5K. Much larger spaces than Paris apartments.

Car Dependency

Most US cities require a car (unlike Paris Metro). Budget $400-800/month for car, insurance, gas.

Lower Taxes in Some States

Texas, Florida, Nevada have NO state income tax (vs. France's high taxation).
